2. Privacy policy

We apply the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD). Data controller: Equipo Los Zenith, contacto@xpaze.es.

What data is processed, why and where

Visiting the website

Your IP address and technical connection data are processed by Cloudflare, the network that delivers the site and protects it from attacks. Our server keeps no visit logs. There is no analytics and no advertising, and fonts and libraries are served from xpaze.es itself (no Google Fonts).

Legal basis: legitimate interest in providing a secure website (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

On your device

Check-up history, chosen profile, language, Paze's memory (session summaries and learned facts), "About me", your medical history and watch pairing codes are stored only in your browser (local storage). We do not receive them, except as described under "AI features". You can delete them in the app ("Clear history", "Forget everything") or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Legal basis: features you request (Art. 6.1.a and 6.1.b GDPR).

AI features

When you ask for a check-up explanation or talk to Paze, our server sends the AI your message and the context it needs: measurements, history and, if you have written them, your medical history and "About me". Providers:

  • Anthropic (Claude): text conversations and explanations.
  • OpenAI: live voice. The audio goes directly from your browser to OpenAI with a short-lived key issued by our server.

Our server does not store conversations. The providers process them under their API terms: by default they are not used to train models, and they may be kept for a limited time for security. Both companies are based in the United States; the transfer relies on the safeguards of Chapter V GDPR offered by these providers.

Legal basis: your consent when you use the feature (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) and, if you enter real health data, your explicit consent (Art. 9.2.a GDPR). Our advice: use the simulated profiles and do not write real health data you do not want to share.

Abuse limits

To prevent abuse of the AI, the server keeps in memory, for 10 minutes, a count of requests per IP address. It is never written to disk.

Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).

Watch data (XPaze Link, optional)

If you use the XPaze Link Android app, it sends Health Connect data (heart rate, sleep, steps…) under a random 8-character code, with no name. It is kept on our server for 48 hours at most and then deleted automatically.

Legal basis: explicit consent (Art. 9.2.a GDPR). You can withdraw it by unlinking the code or uninstalling the app.

Space weather

Our server queries NASA's DONKI service. No data about you is sent.

Recipients, retention and decisions

  • Service providers acting on our behalf: Cloudflare (delivery and security), Anthropic and OpenAI (AI). We never sell or share data for advertising.
  • Retention: conversations are not stored; watch data for up to 48 h; the abuse counter for 10 min; everything else stays on your device until you delete it.
  • No decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made automatically. The traffic light is guidance for the person and their medical team.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to contacto@xpaze.es. As almost everything is stored on your device, you can also delete it yourself. You may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Minors: XPaze is not intended for children under 14.

Security: encrypted connection (https), API keys never reach the browser, and watch data is stored with restricted permissions.

5. Medical disclaimer

XPaze is not a medical device under Regulation (EU) 2017/745. It is not certified or clinically validated, and it does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease. Most of the data is simulated. The thresholds come from general clinical references for adults at rest and are guidance only. Paze is a support AI: it does not replace a health or mental health professional.

In an emergency, call 112. If you are having suicidal thoughts in Spain, call 024 (free, 24/7).

6. Artificial intelligence

  • The app always tells you when you are talking to an AI, in line with the transparency obligations of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 50).
  • The AI never decides the colour of the traffic light: fixed, documented rules decide it, and the AI only explains the result.
  • AI answers can be wrong. Crisis messages always show emergency resources.
  • We declare how we used AI to build XPaze and inside the product: AI use declaration.

Los proveedores las tratan según sus condiciones para la API: por defecto no se usan para entrenar modelos y pueden conservarse un tiempo limitado por seguridad. Límites contra abusos

Para evitar abusos de la IA, el servidor guarda en memoria, durante 10 minutos, un recuento de peticiones por dirección IP. Nunca se escribe en disco.

Base legal: interés legítimo (art. 6.1.f RGPD).
